# pharmacovigilance-task4
## Classification & Severity Analysis

### Objective
Classify adverse drug reactions (ADRs) based on seriousness and expectedness using pharmacovigilance guidelines.

### Case Classification

| Case | Drug | Adverse Reaction | Seriousness | Expectedness | Summary |
|------|------|------------------|-------------|--------------|---------|
| 01 | Amoxicillin | Skin rash, itching | Non-serious | Expected | Skin rash and itching are known adverse reactions associated with amoxicillin. |
| 02 | Ibuprofen | Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization | Serious | Expected | Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization is a serious reaction because it requires hospitalization and is a known risk of ibuprofen. |
| 03 | Metformin | Lactic acidosis | Serious | Expected | Lactic acidosis is a serious and potentially life-threatening adverse reaction associated with metformin. |

### Definitions

**Serious ADR:**  
An adverse drug reaction that results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalization, causes significant disability, or is medically important.

**Non-serious ADR:**  
An adverse reaction that does not meet the criteria for seriousness.

**Expected ADR:**  
A reaction that is consistent with the known safety information, product label, or established characteristics of the drug.

**Unexpected ADR:**  
A reaction whose nature, severity, or specificity is not consistent with the known safety information of the drug.

### Conclusion

The three cases were classified according to seriousness and expectedness.  
Amoxicillin-associated skin rash and itching were classified as non-serious and expected.  
Ibuprofen-associated gastrointestinal bleeding and metformin-associated lactic acidosis were classified as serious and expected adverse reactions.
